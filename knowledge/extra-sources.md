# Extra sources (not from YouTube)

## Alex Hormozi, pinned post in Skool Platinum (August) community, "10 Important Small-Big Reminders", posted 2026-09-01
source_url: https://www.skool.com/platinum-august (pinned post)
1) Don't use a Linktree. Focus all traffic on one thing, your skool.com/xyz link. A confused mind doesn't buy.
2) Do NOT CTA "join my community." Sell the OUTCOME: "First five clients in 7 days", "cut your elbow pain in half in 14 days." CTAs should be "get this outcome."
3) Advertise widgets/courses/trainings but put them all in one place, your Skool. Don't sell them separately; list them as bonuses of joining.
4) Link all your socials.
5) Enable an annual billing option. It will only make you more money.
6) Rotate the widgets you talk about in your marketing. One widget or training per month makes something static seem new.
7) Do not load too much stuff in your community. #1 reason for cancellation is OVERWHELM. When in doubt, leave it out. Value per second, not seconds of value. Most people check 1-3 times a week.
8) Post more content. Actually do this.
9) Make regular CTAs. People will not get tired or offended. If you don't talk about it much they'll think you aren't serious.
10) Recurring revenue takes recurring work. 90% of your work is promotion. Stay consistent.

## Nick Saraev, Skool post "How I won the Skool Games with a $300K/mo, one-man Skool" (2025)
source_url: https://www.skool.com/skoolers (post)
- Show up every day; respond to nearly every new post once every 24 hours in one batch. ~0.021 minutes per member per day.
- Turn a B2C promise ("learn about X") into a B2B promise ("get this specific monetary outcome with X"), ideally with a guarantee ("first customer in 90 days or your money back").
- Launch first, build later: blank community, $97 pre-commit, 20 min of Canva assets, invite ~20 followers asking for feedback so the group isn't empty, price low ($28/mo) then raise as assets are added.
- Minimize calls; use short Loom replies (4-8 per day, ~2 min) instead; they become standalone assets.
- #wins is the most important category; pin 1-2 new wins every 48 hours. Showing, not telling.
- Onboarding via a welcome thread: intro post, comment on 3 posts, read member wins, start the pre-program.
- Classroom structured as daily actionables (Day 1, Day 2...), lock future months by tenure (also reduces churn).
- Show the price history in the About section so early members see their discount.
- Weekly "community exclusive" asset (30 min stream of consciousness on a struggle you saw in replies).
- Batch DMs weekly; ignore distractions; consistency beats strategy; feedback beats planning.
